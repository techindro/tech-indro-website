Frequently Asked Questions
How do I download my verified certificate?
Go to your Student Dashboard, click on "Verified Credentials & Honors", and tap "Download Certificate". You can also directly add it to your LinkedIn profile with one click.
Can I complete hands-on labs on mobile without a laptop?
Yes! All courses, quizzes, and even the IndroLabs Code Playground and ISRO Virtual Space Lab run natively inside your smartphone browser and the mobile app.
What is the selection process for TSOC 2026 Fellowship?
Submit your application from the TSOC page with your GitHub link. Shortlisted applicants are invited for a 15-minute technical conversation and receive 1-on-1 mentorship with ₹50,000 stipend.
Are the Test Series questions aligned with GATE & ISRO patterns?
Yes, our 4,500+ question bank is curated from official GATE, ISRO ICRB, DRDO, and top product company hiring assessments with MCQ, MSQ, and NAT formats.
How does the AI Shikshak & Code Debugger work?
AI Shikshak is powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash and autonomous coding engines. You can ask questions in Hindi, English or Hinglish, and get real-time code diagnosis, bug fixes, and concept explanations 24/7.
