Privacy & Security Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 | Organization: Tech Indro Educational Foundation

1. Zero Credential & Financial Harvesting Guarantee

Tech Indro is an open educational and research initiative. We do not collect, request, or store banking credentials, credit/debit card numbers, UPI PINs, or SMS OTPs. Any educational scholarships or course grants are issued directly without requiring sensitive financial disclosures.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We only process minimal student information necessary to deliver educational content:

  • Account Data: Name and email address provided during voluntary registration.
  • Learning Progress: Quiz scores, coding playground outputs, and bookmark preferences saved locally or on secured databases.
  • Voice Assistant: Mic input used in AI Shikshak is processed strictly via client-side Web Speech APIs with zero voice recordings retained.

3. Data Protection & Security Controls

Our infrastructure utilizes state-of-the-art protections including TLS/HTTPS encryption, strict HTTP Content Security Policies, rate limiting, and sandbox isolation for student code execution.

4. Cookies & Storage Technologies Policy

We employ lightweight HTTP cookies and client-side storage (localStorage) to give you an optimal learning experience:

  • Strictly Necessary: Manages your secure login session, anti-DDoS rate limit protection, and dark/light UI preference.
  • Functional: Retains in-browser IndroLabs code editor drafts, selected programming languages, and quiz progress.
  • Performance & Analytics: Aggregates non-personally identifiable telemetry to minimize page load times and server latency.

You can update or revoke your cookie choices at any time by clicking the floating cookie icon or opening your Cookie Preferences Center.

5. Contact Security Team

For inquiries, security disclosures, or account assistance, contact us at security@techindro.com or visit our Support Desk.
